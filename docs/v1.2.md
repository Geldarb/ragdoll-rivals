# 1.2 — approved hybrid active ragdoll

Bradley approved authored animation targets tracked by physical bodies, with partial/full limp reactions to hits and a blended get-up. This replaces uncoordinated arm torque, not the authoritative collision simulation.

## Implementation decisions

A pure server pose graph generates idle/guard, advance/retreat, weapon-specific wind-up/strike/recovery, shield/parry block, evasive dodge, stagger and get-up targets. All seven styles have explicit cadence, stride, stance/crouch, guard-height, wind-up and lunge parameters. Weight slows gait and lengthens the authored wind-up fraction without changing weapon cooldowns, damage, stamina or the match timeout. Two-dimensional dodges are leans/short retreat steps, not literal depth movement.

The physical rig gains explicit hand bodies. Forearms connect to hands, weapons connect at the visible grip and claymores connect both hands along the hilt. IK solves elbow targets, then bounded PD forces/torques track the authored limb/hand/weapon targets. No normal body positions are teleported. Big-hit reactions reduce drives based on damage, gear weight and balance. Existing stance support also goes limp during stagger, then powers back through get-up. Collisions and momentum can interrupt the pose.

The renderer draws limb endpoints from body transforms and weapons from the real grip, not from unrelated limb centres. Pose state is sent in the existing compact 30 Hz snapshot and interpolated normally. Camera and arena framing remain unchanged.

## Boundaries

This is a stylised two-dimensional active ragdoll, not motion capture. Motors intentionally yield during heavy impact; contacts can disturb limbs and feet. No new services, paid resources, camera zoom, damage tuning or timeout changes.

## Implementation verification

Local Node suite: 41 passed, 0 failed (existing 35 plus six hybrid-pose regressions). Local Playwright: 13 passed, 0 failed, no console errors. Guard envelope covers all five weapon classes for 180 frames. Claymore grip error is tested against Matter's actual rotated grip offset (Matter rotates constraint offsets in place). Added hand bodies bring the connected rig from 14 to 16 bodies.

Reviewed local screenshots at both sizes. In guard the hands/weapons are raised in front; mid-strike the claymore reaches forward from both hand grips; a natural impact produces a collapsing opponent. The first capture accidentally selected the opponent's strike while the player was winding up; the capture predicate now explicitly selects the player's physical forward-facing claymore and a later strike phase. Knockdown capture requires a released-drive pose, torso below y=390 and tilt >1.1 radians, not an injected pose.

The integration suite reproduced a pre-existing disconnect race: combat continued during the grace period and could KO a fighter before the expected disconnect forfeit. Disconnect now stops that room's physics timer during the unchanged ten-second grace period, preserving the documented forfeit outcome.

Visual limits: this is simple vector anatomy. Elbows can pop slightly and overlapping fighters can obscure hands; collisions intentionally disturb the authored pose. The two-dimensional dodge is a lean/retreat, not depth-plane sidestepping. Damage numbers, mitigation, stat rules, weapon cooldowns and timeout are not retuned. Attack phases now align damage eligibility to the authored strike rather than the old fixed countdown window.
